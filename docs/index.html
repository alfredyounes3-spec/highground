FAQ

Questions before you order

What is the difference between a party boost and a piloted boost?

In a party boost you play on your own account and our booster queues with you. In a piloted boost the booster plays on your account. Sharing an account breaks the Steam Subscriber Agreement and can lead to a ban, so we recommend party boosts.

How is the price calculated?

Enter your current number and the one you want in the price calculator at the top of the page, for MMR or behavior score. MMR boosts are priced per 100 MMR and cost more in higher brackets. Calibration and low priority are priced per game, coaching per hour, and some services have a flat price. The order window shows the exact total before you pay.

How can I pay?

Payment is agreed with you on Discord before your order starts. No payment is taken on this website.

When does my order start?

Each service lists a start time, usually between one and six hours after the order is confirmed on Discord. Coaching sessions and Battle Cup stacks are scheduled with you.

Can I get a refund?

Refund terms are agreed with you on Discord before you pay.

Which regions do you cover?

All services run on the European servers, and all communication is in English.
